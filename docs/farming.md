# Title: Farming

The **Farming** module delivers dedicated management tools for island owners, crop growers, herb farmers, animal breeders, and potion/food producers.

---

## Key Pages & Sub-modules

### 1. Farming Dashboard & Island Overview (`/dashboard/farming`)
- **Multi-Island Management**: Overview of personal islands and guild islands with plot capacity and current utilization.
- **Daily Task Tracker**: Checklists for watering, feeding, animal petting, and harvesting rotations.

### 2. Crop & Herb Farming Planner (`/dashboard/farming/crops`)
- **Growth Schedules & Timers**: 22-hour cycle countdown timers for Carrots, Beans, Wheat, Turnips, Cabbage, Potatoes, Corn, and Herbs (Arcane Agaric, Brightleaf Comfrey, Crenellated Burdock, etc.).
- **Watering & Seed Return**: Seed return probability with and without Focus, highlighting self-sustaining seed cycles.
- **Yield Projection**: Estimated total crop yield per 9-plot farm based on active premium status.

### 3. Livestock & Animal Breeding (`/dashboard/farming/livestock`)
- **Animal Growth Stages**: Timers for Chickens, Goats, Geese, Sheep, Pigs, and Cows.
- **Mount Raising**: Growth schedules for Horses, Oxen, Direwolves, Direbears, and Swamp Dragons.
- **Daily Feeding Calculator**: Calculates nutrition required per pasture and compares vegetable costs against produce/meat profits.

### 4. Island Layout & Plot Optimizer (`/dashboard/farming/layout`)
- **Interactive Island Canvas**: 3x3 plot visualizer for mapping Farms, Herb Gardens, Pastures, Houses, and Mills.
- **Upgrade Level Guide**: Island upgrade tiers (Level 1 to Level 6) and unlocks for maximum farming efficiency.

### 5. Cooking & Alchemy Ingredient Planner (`/dashboard/farming/production`)
- **Consumables Pipeline**: Direct ingredient mapping for popular PvP/PvE foods:
  - Beef Stew (Carrots, Raw Beef, Bread)
  - Pork Roast (Raw Pork, Cabbage)
  - Omelettes (Eggs, Milk, Pork)
- **Potion Recipes**: Required herbs and ingredients for Resistance, Healing, Energy, and Poison potions.

### 6. Daily Farming Profitability (`/dashboard/farming/profit`)
- **Net Silver Estimator**: Factors in seed replacement costs, nutrition feed costs, focus point allocation, and local market prices to calculate true daily profit.
