# Title: Maps

The **Maps** module is an interactive tactical atlas for gatherers, gankers, roaming parties, transport caravans, and ZvZ shotcallers across the world of Albion.

---

## Key Pages & Sub-modules

### 1. Interactive World Map (`/dashboard/maps`)
- **Interactive Multi-Tier Canvas**: Zoom, pan, and search across:
  - The Royal Continent
  - The Deep Outlands (Black Zones)
  - Brecilien & The Mists
  - Avalonian Roads
- **Search with Auto-Complete**: Instant search for any zone name (e.g., *Redtree Enclave*, *Runnel Sink*, *Sunfang Ravine*).

### 2. Zone Directory & Danger Filter (`/dashboard/maps/zones`)
- **Lethal Zone Status**: Color-coded badges for Blue (Safe), Yellow (Non-lethal PvP), Red (Full-loot flagged PvP), and Black (Full-loot Outlands).
- **Tier & Biome Classifications**: Filter by Tier (T3 through T8) and Biome (Forest, Steppe, Swamp, Mountain, Highlands).

### 3. Resource Node Density & Gathering Hotspots (`/dashboard/maps/resources`)
- **Node Distribution Overlays**: Heatmap layers for:
  - Wood (Forests)
  - Ore (Highlands / Mountains)
  - Fiber (Swamps)
  - Hide (Steppes)
  - Stone (Highlands)
- **Enchanted Spawns**: High-density zones for Tier 6.x, 7.x, and 8.x resources.

### 4. Avalonian Roads & Portal Tracker (`/dashboard/maps/roads`)
- **Portal Connection Mapping**: Green (Solo/2-man), Blue (7-man), and Golden (20-man) portal connections.
- **Expiration Timers**: Time remaining before a road portal closes or resets connection.
- **Road Resting Zones**: Identifies zones with player hideouts and rest sanctuaries.

### 5. Static Dungeons & Boss Spawns (`/dashboard/maps/dungeons`)
- **Group Dungeon Locations**: T6-T8 static dungeons, faction alignments (Heretic, Morgana, Undead, Keeper), and mob densities.
- **World Boss & Aspect Timers**: Respawn schedules and coordinates for Resource Aspects (Mammoths, Old White, Dryads) and World Bosses.

### 6. Territory, Castles & Outpost Timers (`/dashboard/maps/territories`)
- **Vortex & Core Spawns**: Active Power Core and Energy Vortex timers and map locations.
- **Castle & Outpost Scoring**: Battle timers and point yields for active guild season standings.
