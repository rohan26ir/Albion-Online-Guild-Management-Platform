# Title: Others (Player Demand Features)

The **Others** module houses dynamic, community-requested tools and gameplay utilities developed directly in response to active player feedback and community voting.

---

## Key Pages & Sub-modules

### 1. Character Build Library & Loadout Creator (`/dashboard/builds`)
- **Visual Equipment Slots**: Head, Chest, Shoes, Main Hand, Off Hand, Cape, Food, Potion, and Mount.
- **Spell & Passive Selector**: Visual skill picker displaying Q, W, E, and Passive spell options.
- **Activity Tagging**: Filter builds by activity:
  - Solo PvP / Corrupted Dungeons
  - Small Scale / Roaming
  - ZvZ (Zerg vs Zerg)
  - Solo & Group PvE (HCE, Static Dungeons, Avalonian Raids)
  - Gathering & Escape Sets
- **Ratings & Community Comments**: Upvote builds, write reviews, and share direct loadout links.

### 2. PvP Tracker & Killboard (`/dashboard/pvp`)
- **Battle & Kill Reports**: Integrates with official Albion Online kill feeds to display recent kills and deaths.
- **Kill Fame Analysis**: Fame gained/lost, item power (IP) differential between combatants, and inventory loot value.
- **Guild & Alliance Leaderboards**: Tracks top PvP performers and guild K/D ratios.

### 3. Gathering Route Planner & Yield Logger (`/dashboard/gathering`)
- **Gathering Log**: Personal logbook tracking gathering hours, node tier breakdowns, and silver earned per hour.
- **Aspect & Node Respawn Timers**: Timer tracking for major resource nodes and aspect kills.

### 4. Guides, Tutorials & Meta Strategies (`/dashboard/guides`)
- **Beginner Essentials**: Guides for new recruits on economy, faction warfare, and survival.
- **Advanced Mechanics**: Focus point strategies, Black Zone survival guides, shotcalling primers, and market flipping tutorials.
- **Weapon Tier Lists**: Regularly updated meta tier lists for Corrupted Dungeons, Hellgates, and Mists.

### 5. Player Profiles & Achievements (`/dashboard/profile`)
- **Player Showcase**: Connected character profile, equipped gear preview, guild history, and achievement highlights.

### 6. Community Demand & Feature Voting (`/dashboard/demand`)
- **Community Idea Submissions**: Space for players to suggest new calculators, trackers, or map tools.
- **Upvoting System**: Players vote on top priority features to guide upcoming platform updates.
