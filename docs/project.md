# Albion Game - Marketplace, Guild and Others

## Project Overview

**Albion Game - Marketplace, Guild and Others** is an all-in-one comprehensive web platform designed specifically for the Albion Online community. Originally conceived as a guild portal, the platform has expanded into an interconnected ecosystem serving everyday players, traders, crafters, gatherers, farmers, shotcallers, guild officers, and alliance leaders.

The platform centralizes real-time market data, in-depth crafting and farming planning tools, interactive maps, guild and alliance operations, precision economy calculators, and player-driven utilities into a unified, high-performance interface.

---

## Core Objectives

- **Empower Economic Mastery**: Provide live market prices across all major royal cities, Caerleon, Brecilien, and the Black Market, alongside arbitrage and margin finders.
- **Optimize Crafting & Refining**: Calculate exact Resource Return Rates (RRR), focus point savings, nutrition costs, and station fees for maximum silver generation.
- **Streamline Island & Farming Operations**: Give farmers and breeders dedicated island planners, harvest rotation schedulers, and animal breeding margin tools.
- **Enhance Tactical Navigation & Gathering**: Deliver responsive interactive maps for the Royal Continent, Outlands, Mists, and Avalonian Roads with resource density and danger ratings.
- **Unify Guild & Alliance Operations**: Simplify recruitment workflows, roster management, role permissions, Call To Arms (CTA) tracking, and treasury auditing.
- **Provide Precision Calculators**: Offer specialized calculators for refining, crafting, market taxes, transport trade runs, fame/spec optimization, and focus efficiency.
- **Iterate on Player Demands**: Dynamically adapt to community needs with player build creators, killboard trackers, gathering route logs, and guides.

## Detailed Section Specifications

### 1. Title: Market Price
Dedicated to real-time and historical economic data across Albion's decentralized markets:
- **Live Price Checker**: Instant price lookup across Martlock, Bridgewatch, Lymhurst, Fort Sterling, Thetford, Caerleon, and Brecilien.
- **Black Market Tracker**: Identify high-margin arbitrage opportunities between the Royal continent craft hubs and the Caerleon Black Market.
- **Price History & Graphical Trends**: Candlestick and line charts tracking item valuations over 24-hour, 7-day, 30-day, and monthly intervals.
- **Order Spread & Volume**: Visual analysis of bid-ask spreads, liquidity, and velocity of high-demand gear, consumables, and mounts.
- **Price Alerts & Watchlist**: Custom player alerts for market dips, price spikes, and profitable buy order fulfillment.

### 2. Title: Crafting
Engineered for dedicated crafters and production magnates:
- **Station Fee & Usage Tracker**: Compare associate rates and public food taxes across city crafting stations.
- **Resource Return Rate (RRR) Analyzer**: Dynamic calculations including daily local production bonuses, city-specific crafting bonuses, and Focus boosts (up to 53.9%+ return).
- **Recipe & Tier Explorer**: Full breakdown of required refined materials, artifacts, journals, and food costs.
- **Focus Efficiency Calculator**: Spec-based focus cost reduction curves for weapons, armor, tools, and accessories.
- **Labourer Management**: Tracking journal filling, labourer happiness, house tiers, furniture requirements, and daily return rates.
- **Transmutation & Enchanting**: Real-time evaluation of whether transmuting raw materials or enchanting items with runes, souls, and relics is profitable.

### 3. Title: Farming
Tailored for island owners, breeders, cooks, and alchemists:
- **Crop & Herb Planner**: Growth timers, watering requirements, seed return probabilities, and crop yields per plot.
- **Livestock & Pasture Manager**: Baby animal growth stages, daily nutrition consumption (carrots, beans, etc.), animal produce (milk, eggs), and meat harvesting margins.
- **Island Layout Optimizer**: Plot allocation visualizer for Personal Islands and Guild Islands (farming plots vs building plots).
- **Farming Net Margin Calculator**: Compares seed costs, focus watering savings, and daily harvest sales to determine net silver per day.
- **Consumables Pipeline**: Direct ingredient mapping for popular food (e.g., Beef Stew, Pork Roast) and potions (e.g., Gigantify, Resistance, Poison).

### 4. Title: Maps
A tactical intelligence tool for gatherers, gankers, roaming parties, and ZvZ shotcallers:
- **Interactive Global Map**: Seamless zooming between the Royal Continent, Deep Outlands, Avalonian Roads, and Brecilien / Mists.
- **Zone Danger & Tier Filtering**: Filter zones by Tier (T3 to T8), lethal status (Blue, Yellow, Red, Black), and biome (Forest, Steppe, Swamp, Mountain, Highlands).
- **Resource Heatmaps**: Highlight high-density spawns for Ore, Fiber, Wood, Hide, and Stone, as well as enchanted node hotspots (x.1, x.2, x.3, x.4).
- **Avalonian Roads Mapper**: Mapping green, blue, and golden portals, rest zones, chests, and connecting portal timers.
- **World Timers**: Real-time countdowns for Energy Vortexes, Cores, Castles, Outposts, and World Boss spawns.

### 5. Title: Guild
The operational command center for guild leaders, officers, and members:
- **Roster & Member Directory**: Detailed member listings, online status, guild rank, join date, and total fame contributions (PvP, PvE, Crafting, Gathering).
- **Role & Permission Management**: Granular permissions for guild management, recruitment reviews, announcement authoring, and treasury visibility.
- **Recruitment & Application Queue**: Application forms with Discord tags, character specs, screenshot verification, and quick review status (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
- **Call To Arms (CTA) & Event Coordinator**: Mandatory and optional CTA schedules, gear requirements, party compositions, and automated attendance logging.
- **Guild Statistics**: Guild Season point progression, PvE/PvP fame leaderboards, territory control, and activity heatmaps.
- **Alliance Hub**: Multi-guild announcements, shared Intel channels, and coordinated defensive schedules.
- **Treasury & Tax Tracker**: Guild tax rates, donation logs, and island upkeep expenses.

### 6. Title: Calculation
Precision mathematical models for economic decision making:
- **Crafting Profit Calculator**: Real-time net margin estimator factoring in material costs, RRR, nutrition taxes, journal buy/sell rates, and market fees.
- **Refining Calculator**: Step-by-step raw resource processing profitability (Tier 2 up to Tier 8.4) with city refining bonus toggles.
- **Fame & Spec Calculator**: Calculates the exact number of tomes of insight, mob fame, or crafting actions needed to hit level 100 or 120 mastery/specialization.
- **Market Tax & Setup Fee Calculator**: Computes exact net silver after premium market tax (4%), non-premium tax (8%), and order setup fees (2.5%).
- **Transport Arbitrage Calculator**: Models transport runs across the continent (e.g., Caerleon to Royal cities), factoring in mount carry weight, ox speed, item volume, and risk vs reward.
- **Focus Efficiency Optimizer**: Daily 10,000 focus allocation advisor calculating highest silver-per-focus-point return.

### 7. Others (Player Demand Features)
Flexible community-driven modules built directly from player feedback:
- **Character Build Library**: Interactive build creator with gear slots (Head, Chest, Shoes, Main Hand, Off Hand, Cape, Food, Potion, Mount), spell selector, and role categorization (PvP, PvE, Solo, ZvZ, Small Scale, Gathering).
- **PvP Tracker & Killboard**: Integration with official Albion killboards for battle reports, kill/death ratios, fame gained/lost, and gear loot values.
- **Gathering Log & Route Planner**: Tools for gatherers to track node cycles, aspect spawns, and silver-per-hour benchmarks.
- **Knowledge Base & Strategy Guides**: Structured guides for beginners, weapon tier lists, economy secrets, and corrupted dungeon strategies.
- **Player Feedback & Feature Voting**: An interactive polling system allowing active players to submit and upvote new tool concepts for subsequent platform releases.

## Technical Architecture & Design Principles

- **Framework**: Next.js 16 (App Router) with React 19 and TypeScript.
- **Styling & Design System**: Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui components, Radix UI primitives, crisp border system (`--radius: 0`), and responsive dark/light mode tokens in `app/globals.css`.
- **Data Visualization**: Chart.js and react-chartjs-2 for market depth, price histories, and guild fame statistics.
- **Real-time & State**: Zustand for fast client state (calculator inputs, filter preferences), Supabase client for database/auth workflows.
- **Icons & Micro-Interactions**: Tabler Icons React, Lucide, Framer Motion, and Lottie animations for an engaging, game-inspired interface.
- **Performance & SEO**: Server-side rendering for public content, metadata optimization, and low-latency client calculators.
